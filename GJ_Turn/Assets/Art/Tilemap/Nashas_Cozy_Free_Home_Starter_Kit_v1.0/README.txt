NASHA'S COZY WORLD: FREE HOME STARTER KIT
=========================================

Hi, and welcome home!

This is a free starter kit: everything you need to build one cozy
little home, from the front garden to the bathroom. 111 objects,
128 colour variants and 35 tiles - 274 files in all.

Every sprite comes from the paid Cozy World packs, picked so that a
whole house fits together: a living room, a bedroom, a bathroom, a
kitchen with food on the counter and a garden outside.


WHERE THINGS ARE
----------------

sprites/        One PNG per object, sorted by room: living_room,
                bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, food, garden_and_outside
                and decor. Transparent background, trimmed tight.

  .../variants/ Sofas, rugs, beds and wooden furniture in a few extra
                colours and wood tones.

spritesheets/   Each folder on a single sheet with equal-sized cells. The
                .json next to each sheet tells you which cell is which.

tiles/          Seamless 32x32 tiles: wooden and parquet floors, bathroom
                and checker tiles, wallpapers, a garden lawn, a gravel
                path and flower bed soil, plus lawn edge tiles.

palette/        The 128-colour Cozy World palette (.hex and .gpl).

manifest.json   A list of every object with its room and size.


A FEW TIPS
----------

The sprites are small on purpose, so you can scale them to suit your
game. Scale by whole numbers - 2x, 3x, 4x - and turn off smoothing so
the pixels stay crisp:

  Godot       set the texture Filter to Off (or Nearest)
  Unity       Filter Mode: Point, Compression: None
  GameMaker   untick "Interpolate colours between pixels"


USING THE PACK
--------------

It's free! Use it in your free and commercial games and projects, and
edit it however you like.

Please don't resell or re-upload the sprites themselves as an asset
pack, and don't use them to train AI models. Full terms in LICENSE.txt.


Want more rooms? The Cozy World Mega Pack has every Cozy World pack in
one download - homes, cafes, shops, villages, gardens, forests and
seasons, all in this same style and scale.

Have fun!
