# Modern Home & Apartment - Free Sample (Chucky Assets)

22 tiles and props from **Modern Home & Apartment**, the 16x16 top-down home interior tileset: a sofa, armchair, coffee
table, animated TV on a stand, floor lamp, a cat in its bed (animated), double bed, nightstand lamp, kitchen counters with an
animated stove and sink, fridge, dining table and chair, toilet, bathtub, monstera, oak and kitchen floors, a wall and an
animated door.

- Single PNG sprites at **16, 32 and 48 px**, with and without shadow (`sprites/<variant>/<size>/<category>/`).
- Animated items also come as `_anim<N>f.png` strips (frames side by side).
- Works in Godot, Unity, GameMaker, RPG Maker and Tiled. Use nearest-neighbour filtering.

The full pack has 263 tiles and props (35 animated): living room, kitchen, dining, bedroom, bathroom, kids' room, home office,
laundry and hallway, two sample maps and Tiled / Godot 4 / RPG Maker MZ files:
